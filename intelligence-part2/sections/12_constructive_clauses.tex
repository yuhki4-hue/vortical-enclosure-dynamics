\section{Constructive Clauses}

\noindent\textbf{Scope of the account.} These clauses organize a model-local architecture and proposed generalizations. Their necessity labels are not universal necessity theorems. The sapiens profile adds endogenous mobility; this is not a condition for intelligence in general. The six/seven-clause and network/external-stop scope differences remain unresolved rather than silently unified. Free inference is a functional interpretation, not a phenomenal or normative consequence. Independent criteria for general intelligence, error tolerance, and freedom remain open; the field/network identity remains unresolved.

The log and stopping clauses below retain the old architecture's constructive proposals. Section 5's active role account distinguishes how a support is assessed; it neither proves these clauses necessary for general intelligence nor adds a scaffold regime as a new universal condition. The existing sapiens comparison and unresolved clause and network scopes remain separate.


This chapter summarizes the structural conditions proposed in the paper. These clauses are not normative prescriptions. They organize proposed model conditions rather than consequences establishing universal necessity.

\subsection{Clause 1: Inference Alone Does Not Close}

The proposed architecture distinguishes intelligence from $L_F$ alone. The historical local-state model's no-fixed-point discussion of $F$ is conditional; it does not exclude every independently defined intelligence system or cognitive stopping process.

\subsection{Clause 2: Logs Are Required}

Recursive traces are part of the proposed stopping architecture. The role assigned to $L_R$ does not establish a universal emergence condition or supply an existence result.

\subsection{Clause 3: Stopping Is Externally Dependent}

The relation of $L_E$ to $L_R$ under external mutual constraint $H_{ij}$ is an emergence hypothesis. Internal location and external dependency describe its proposed functional role, not an established existence or convergence result.

\subsection{Clause 4: Sapiens-Like Intelligence Requires $\mu_\theta$}

Intelligence in general does not require endogenous $\mu_\theta$. Sapiens-like intelligence does. It can reorganize its own movement region and relocate stopping operators.

\subsection{Clause 5: A Proposed Relation Between Stability and Revisability}

Stable intelligence is not guaranteed by correctness. A relation to revisability is proposed. Rigidity and divergence are qualitative hypotheses, not established consequences of the naming alone.

\subsection{Clause 6: Social Stopping Is Networked}

The external reference field is not an isolated transcendent object. It is proposed as a view of inter-individual $L_E$ synchronization; the stronger identity below remains unresolved:
\[
\Phi_{\text{ext}}\equiv\Phi_{\text{net}}.
\]

\subsection{Clause 7: Movable Stops as a Proposal for Open Intelligence}

The final constructive proposal relates free inference to movable stopping without establishing a universal or phenomenal necessity. Regress or explosion without stopping, and rigidity without mobility, are qualitative hypotheses within this proposal. They are not independent evidence for a universal persistence criterion.
