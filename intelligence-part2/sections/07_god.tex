\section{God: Compression of Distributed Stopping}

\noindent\textbf{Scope of the account.} God is interpreted here through the proposed function of suspension and central reference, without an ontological assertion. Centralization reducing ambiguity or stabilizing stopping is a structural hypothesis, not a network theorem or a validated historical cause. The single-reference pole is not a necessary historical endpoint; cost, ambiguity, and field/network correspondence remain unresolved.

The centralized or shared reference discussed here can be compared with retention roles under Section 5's scope convention, but centralization or compression alone does not identify a single support, certify LE, or establish a subject or owner. The existing interpretation of God remains a conceptual proposal; neither an E mechanism nor a field/network map is supplied by this role comparison.


If animism distributes stopping across many environmental loci, the figure of God compresses stopping into a central reference point. The function analyzed here is not theological proof or disproof, but structural compression.

Distributed stopping is flexible but ambiguous. Many loci can conflict. A centralized stopping point is hypothesized to reduce ambiguity by gathering multiple stopping functions into one reference. In the language of this paper, God is a compressed $\Phi_{\text{ext}}$.

This does not make God the historical endpoint of a progress narrative. God is treated here as a structural pole of compression: one possible operation by which distributed stopping is gathered, weighted, and stabilized.

This compression is hypothesized to stabilize shared reference. It is interpreted as providing a unified answer to otherwise silent questions. It can close regress, coordinate communities, and bind action to a common orientation.

Yet compression also carries risk. If the compressed stopping point becomes too rigid, it can produce hard closure. The problem is not that stopping exists, but that stopping loses revisability. The later distinction between religion, science, and social blockchain can be read as different responses to this risk.

God is therefore positioned as an operation in the history of stopping structures:
\[
\text{distributed stopping} \;\longrightarrow\; \text{centralized stopping}.
\]
