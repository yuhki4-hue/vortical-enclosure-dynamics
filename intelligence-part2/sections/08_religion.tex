\section{Religion: Long-Term Maintenance of Stopping Structures}

\noindent\textbf{Scope of the account.} Religion as maintenance and science as revision are conceptual reinterpretations, not empirical definitions of either institution. Effects on persistence, burden, or rigidity are hypotheses requiring independent historical criteria. They do not establish a general social law or a clinical classification. The nonhierarchical truth comparison and negative-scope guards are retained.

For the architectural comparisons in this chapter, intergenerational maintenance is not by itself evidence for a quasi-stable role or ownness. The scope, recipient, re-reference path, and criterion remain those required in Section 5, or Unknown. This qualification concerns role attribution, not a replacement of the existing interpretations of religion, science, or rigidity.


Religion is conceptually reinterpreted as long-term maintenance of stopping structures. Persistence across generations, communities, rituals, texts, and institutions is a hypothesis separate from that naming.

Durability is a proposed function in this reinterpretation. Ritual repetition, texts, social enforcement, and institutional transmission are comparison candidates, not evidence here for a general causal law of persistence.

Religion and the intergenerational availability of $L_E$ are compared as roles. Whether such maintenance actually prevents regress requires criteria beyond the comparison.

This maintenance can be constructive. It is hypothesized to limit endless questioning, without an independent causal test here. It can preserve shared orientation, responsibility, and collective memory. But it can also harden. If the stopping structure loses revisability, religion can enter rigid attractors: absolutism, private closure, or refusal of new response.

The structural point is value-neutral. Religion is neither reduced to error nor elevated above critique. It is an operating form of stopping maintenance:
\[
\text{centralized stopping} \;\longrightarrow\; \text{maintained stopping}.
\]

This prepares the role of science. If religion strongly maintains stopping, science institutionalizes the revision of stopping.
