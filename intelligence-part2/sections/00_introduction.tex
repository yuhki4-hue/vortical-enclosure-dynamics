\section{Introduction: A Proposed Stopping Architecture}

\noindent\textbf{Scope of the account.} Part II is constructed using VED/IFGT vocabulary, not established as a typed derivation from the parent theories. The three layers form a proposed model-local architecture; stopping generation is a separate hypothesis. The exact four-layer inheritance attributed to Part I is unresolved: the present descriptions should not be treated as establishing which earlier structure is inherited. Historical categories below are conceptual reinterpretations or historical hypotheses, not validated social laws. Explanatory use of "stopping" here names coarse-grained local retention or residence for a declared object, scale, and time window, not the disappearance of all material or environmental updating. Retained traces, recipient access, actual re-reference or reuse, and effects on judgment remain distinct questions.

For architecture terminology throughout this introduction, the current account in Section 5 separates tracked supports, scoped roles, and temporary regimes. Layer names remain functional shorthand or vocabulary of the locally identified historical proposals. The outline, comparisons, and Part I references do not independently certify retention, formation, or system ownership; this distinction does not withdraw the other conceptual interpretations in the introduction.


This paper is Intelligence Part II in the VED/IFGT theory tree. Part I described intelligence as a non-closed dynamical phenomenon. Part II asks a more specific question: if inference does not close itself, how can intelligence nevertheless stop, judge, act, and persist?

The answer developed here is that intelligence is not merely the growth of inference. Intelligence is the co-evolution of inference and stopping. In the historical local-state model retained in Sections 2--3, the inference layer $L_F$ expands justification, generates branches, and moves along gradients of closure. The fixed-point discussion applies only under the formal premises stated later. If inference is left to inference alone, it can enter infinite regress, branching explosion, or premature pathological closure.

The chain below preserves the historical mechanism proposal. Its arrows are not role-promotion laws, and the active support-role account of Section 5 does not supply a derivation of stopping or emergence from that chain.

The central structure of this paper is therefore:
\[
L_F \;\longrightarrow\; L_R \;\longrightarrow\; L_E .
\]
The inference layer $L_F$ is associated with traces and a recursive log layer $L_R$. The relation of external mutual constraint $H_{ij}$ and the motion of $L_R$ to a stopping layer $L_E$ is an emergence hypothesis. Internal location and external dependency describe a functional role; histories and shared reference do not by themselves establish its existence or convergence.

\subsection*{Four Declarations of Position}
\addcontentsline{toc}{subsection}{Four Declarations of Position}

\textbf{First, this paper is not a theory of religion, culture, science, or society as such.}
Animism, God, religion, science, institutions, and social blockchain appear in this paper only as historical operating forms of stopping structures. The object of analysis is the emergent stopping layer $L_E$ and the ways in which finite intelligence maintains, revises, and synchronizes stopping.

\textbf{Second, religion and science are not compared as superior or inferior truth systems.}
Religion is treated as a large-scale system for the long-term maintenance of stopping structures. Science is treated as a system that institutionalizes the revision of stopping structures. The contrast is not truth versus error, but different modes of stopping operation.

\textbf{Third, stopping is not the negation of freedom.}
$L_E$ is proposed as a resource for operating open-ended inference under finite constraints. This does not establish that stopping is necessary for freedom, or that its absence entails cognitive regress and explosion.

\textbf{Fourth, projection is used structurally.}
When this paper speaks of projection, it does not primarily mean psychological projection. It means a structural operation by which a state, relation, or representation acquires unequal influence within a field. In Intelligence Part II, projection appears as the projection of possible stopping loci.

\subsection{What This Paper Does Not Claim}

This paper does not claim that religion is reducible to a mistake, that science is simply a replacement for religion, or that society is a blockchain in the technical sense. It also does not prescribe how artificial intelligence should be designed or regulated. The argument is descriptive and dynamical. It identifies structural conditions under which inference can persist without closing absolutely.

\subsection{How to Read the History of Intelligence}

In that historical model, the inference operator $F$ is discussed under strict-increase and fixed-region premises. Relating that conditional model to the history of intelligence or to branching and cognitive stability is a separate hypothesis, not a consequence of nonclosure alone.

Finite resources motivate considering inference and stopping together. The proposed co-evolution account does not establish that every cognitive system without a specified stopping structure must be unsustainable.

Animism, God, religion, science, institutions, and social blockchain are not opposed objects. They are historical operating forms for sharing, maintaining, revising, and synchronizing the emergent stopping layer $L_E$. In this sense, the history of intelligence is a history of stopping structures.
