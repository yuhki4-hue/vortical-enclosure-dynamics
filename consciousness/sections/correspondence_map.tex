\textbf{Reading suggestions:}

For readers who read this paper first: compare the proposed temporal
conditions of conscious opening with CT's conceptual terrain formation.
The geometry vocabulary offers an analogy for referencing bias, not an
established determination of conscious content by a CT field.

For readers who read CT first: SSO offers a comparative temporal architecture
for discussing referenceability. This reading does not make SSO a derived
or independently established necessary condition of CT terrain formation.
The qualia/insight row is likewise an analogy, not CT support for a
phenomenal criterion or the identity of two experiences.

Mutual reading extends the comparison to the VED physical layer, the
Bio-IFGT biological layer, and the intelligence layer \cite{Intelligence}.
Four projected windows can be compared through recurring non-closure and
differentiation motifs without reduction, mathematical isomorphism, or a
shared generative mechanism. The Consciousness-to-self-to-Intelligence
sequence is a conceptual handoff and explanatory order, not a universal
developmental sequence or generation proof. Downstream reuse does not
validate this paper; upstream closure supplies no missing bridge.
