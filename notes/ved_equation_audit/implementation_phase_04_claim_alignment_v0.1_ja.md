- Positive \(\sigma\) is not presented as a necessary-and-sufficient physical-time criterion.
- The \(\sigma=0\) source-free regime is not used to settle whether physical
  conservation laws are fundamental or emergent.
- Difference-to-flow wording is conditional on the stated representation.
- Fixed-point and vorticity constructions no longer assert physical persistence by themselves.
- Triangular minimality is bounded to the stated finite closure class.
- Particle and mass expressions use model-defined / particle-like / phenomenological scope.
- Network distance and effective metric are separate from physical-space emergence.
- Einstein and Schwarzschild statements are comparison targets, not exact recovery claims.
- Gravity, spacetime, black-hole and cosmological readings are candidate interpretations.
- IFGT remains a related-branch proposed correspondence, not evidence of VED identity or correctness.
