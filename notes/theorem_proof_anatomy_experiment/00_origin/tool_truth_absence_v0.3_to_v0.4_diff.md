## 4. Not adopted

### Deferred Resolution

Not adopted as a concept, mechanism, edge type, or open research program. It appears only in:

- the “Not adopted” change log;
- the frozen negative calibration;
- withdrawn-claim and self-audit records.

### Boundary-relocation mechanism

Not added to the assurance network. GST Case 01 showed that apparent residuals divided into model assumptions, nuisance/reference uncertainty, quotient redundancy, reporting choices, misspecification, and model-specific extensions.

### DR taxonomy

Not copied into v0.4. The old scale mixed recurrence, formal invariance, diagnostic utility, and modal impossibility.

### Generalization from GST

The case establishes only that the generic vocabulary failed to add content **in the GST case**. It does not establish that all cross-domain vocabulary is dispensable or that assurance provenance must fail elsewhere.
