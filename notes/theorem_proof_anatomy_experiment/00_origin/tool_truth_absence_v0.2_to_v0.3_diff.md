## 4. Preserved structure audit

The following v0.2 structures were deliberately retained:

- Phase 0–9 and each phase's hypothesis, attraction, counterexample/prior theory, withdrawal, residue, and next question.
- The distinction between truth-existence neutrality and ontological non-self-certification.
- The rejection of universal internal-observer impossibility.
- The distinctions CIF vs preparation/initial independence vs experimental composition.
- Informational refinement vs physical joint realizability.
- Within-model identifiability vs model-class adequacy.
- “Can claim / cannot claim,” VED independence, revision protocol, and withdrawal log.

No v0.2 file was overwritten. The v0.3 changes are additive, reductive, or reclassifying; they do not revive a withdrawn universal theorem.

## 6. Final revision judgment

The strongest change is not an additional claim but a relocation of ownership: the scientific content remains with tomography, calibration/GST, Bell methodology, contextuality, model comparison, statistics, metrology, and philosophy of science. v0.3 claims at most a provisional way to index where their assurances originate, where they are backgrounded, how they are handed off, and which failures cut across them.

The revision therefore decreases theoretical and methodological claim strength while increasing the traceability of scope, provenance, and correction history.
