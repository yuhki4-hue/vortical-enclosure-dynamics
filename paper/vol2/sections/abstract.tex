Vortical Enclosure Dynamics (VED) proposes formal constructions and candidate
physical interpretations from a single axiom:
\emph{there is difference}.
Vol.~1 established the causal log $C_{ij}$ as the foundational variable and
constructed a time-like accumulation quantity $\tau$ and network distance
$d_{ij}$; their physical identifications remain proposed.

This volume extends the closure framework to the internal structure of the Standard Model.
Beginning from the closure energy functional and the minimal condition
for structural persistence, we propose structural comparisons in which the gauge group
$\mathrm{SU}(3)\times\mathrm{SU}(2)\times\mathrm{U}(1)$,
particle classification, generation structure, electric charge,
the weak interaction, and the Higgs mechanism
are related to closure geometry. These are model-level interpretations rather
than observational identifications or universal derivations.

No additional structures are introduced beyond the closure framework.
The present theory does not yet possess quantitative predictive power;
structural organization is the primary goal.
