\section{The Differential Horizon Principle}

\label{sec:principle}

From the preceding discussion,
a fundamental structure emerges.

\medskip
\begin{quote}
\textbf{\DHP{}:}\\
The \ungenerated{} domain is in principle unreachable;
approach to its boundary produces divergence;
and renormalization converts that contact into finite representation.
\end{quote}
\medskip

This principle can be expressed in three propositions.

\begin{enumerate}
  \item The \ungenerated{} domain is unreachable.
  \item Divergence appears at the boundary of approach.
  \item Renormalization converts \bcontact{} into finite form.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection*{The Chain of Necessity}

These propositions are not independent.

\medskip
\begin{quote}
They form a necessary structural chain.
\end{quote}
\medskip

Because the \ungenerated{} domain cannot be described,
approach to its boundary appears as divergence.

Because divergence cannot be handled directly,
it is transformed through renormalization.

\medskip
\begin{quote}
Physical theory operates only within this structure.
\end{quote}
\medskip

\subsection*{What the Principle Reveals}

This principle does not introduce a new theory.

\medskip
\begin{quote}
It reveals the structural constraint
already present in existing theories.
\end{quote}
\medskip

Divergence, renormalization, singularities,
and scale limits are not anomalies.

They are expressions of this principle.

\medskip
\begin{quote}
The \ungenerated{} is not an object.\\
It appears only as a boundary.
\end{quote}
\medskip

\subsection*{Scope and Limit}

The \DHP{} does not explain existence.

\medskip
\begin{quote}
It defines the condition under which explanation becomes possible.
\end{quote}
\medskip

It does not introduce new entities.
It does not propose a unified theory of interactions.
It does not describe what lies beyond the horizon.

What it does is identify the prior structural layer
within which all physical description holds.

\medskip
\begin{quote}
The \DHP{} is not a theory of what exists,\\
but a principle of how existence becomes describable.
\end{quote}
\medskip
