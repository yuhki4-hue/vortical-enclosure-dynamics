\section{Unification of Unsolved Problems}

\label{sec:unification}

Modern physics contains many unsolved problems.

These are typically treated as independent issues
belonging to different domains.

However, from the perspective of the \DH{},
a different view emerges.

\medskip
\begin{quote}
The unsolved problems of modern physics are not independent problems,\\
but different manifestations of a single structural condition.
\end{quote}

\begin{quote}
Each appears when a theory is pushed to its limit of applicability.
\end{quote}
\medskip

\subsection{Divergence in Quantum Field Theory}

At high energies,
quantum field theory exhibits divergences.

This reflects approach toward the \DH{}
through spatial convergence.

\subsection{Renormalization}

Renormalization converts divergence
into finite observable quantities.

It is the mechanism by which \bcontact{}
is incorporated into theory.

\subsection{Planck-Scale Breakdown}

At the Planck scale,
spacetime continuity loses meaning.

Distance and time cease to be definable.

This represents the limit of spatial convergence.

\subsection{The Speed-of-Light Limit}

As velocity approaches the speed of light,
time dilation becomes extreme
and temporal duration tends toward disappearance.

This represents the limit of temporal convergence.

\subsection{Black Hole Singularities}

Under extreme gravitational conditions,
spacetime structure breaks down,
and curvature diverges.

\medskip
\begin{quote}
Black holes represent explicit \bcontact{}.
\end{quote}
\medskip

\subsection{The Quantum Gravity Problem}

as the problem of unifying quantum theory and relativity.

From this perspective,
its meaning shifts.

\medskip
\begin{quote}
The difficulty of quantum gravity reflects the impossibility\\
of extending post-generated frameworks beyond their domain.
