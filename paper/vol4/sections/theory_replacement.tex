\section{Theoretical Re-placement}

\label{sec:replacement}

From the preceding discussion,
the structure of the \DH{}
and the meaning of divergence and renormalization
have become clear.

From this perspective,
modern physical theories can be reorganized.

\medskip
\begin{quote}
Theories are not classified by their objects,\\
but by their mode of approach to the \DH{}.
\end{quote}

\begin{quote}
All theories are positioned within a continuous structure of approach.
\end{quote}
\medskip

\subsection{Quantum Field Theory}

Quantum field theory approaches the \DH{}
through spatial convergence.

At high energies,
field fluctuations increase,
and calculations exhibit divergence.

These divergences are handled through renormalization,
allowing finite predictions.

\medskip
\begin{quote}
Quantum field theory is positioned just before the horizon,
within a domain strongly influenced by \bcontact{}.
\end{quote}
\medskip

\subsection{Relativity}

Relativity approaches the \DH{}
through temporal convergence.

As velocity approaches the speed of light,
time dilates and tends toward cessation.

Under strong gravitational fields,
spacetime structure breaks down,
and singularities appear.

\medskip
\begin{quote}
Relativity approaches the same boundary
through a different mode of convergence.
\end{quote}
\medskip

\subsection{Black Holes}

Black holes represent the most explicit manifestation of \bcontact{}.

At the event horizon,
the structure of spacetime becomes inaccessible to external observers.

At the singularity,
curvature diverges,
and spacetime loses definability.

\medskip
\begin{quote}
Black holes occupy the region where \bcontact{} becomes manifest.
\end{quote}
\medskip

\subsection{String Theory}

String theory emerges as an attempt
to unify quantum field theory and gravity.

Its mathematical structure
seeks to suppress divergence
and maintain internal consistency.

\medskip
\begin{quote}
String theory does not directly touch the boundary,\\
but explores coherence near the limit of approach.
\end{quote}
\medskip

\subsection{Quantum Gravity}

Quantum gravity is often described
as the problem of unifying quantum theory and relativity.

From the perspective of the \DH{},
its meaning shifts.

\medskip
\begin{quote}
Quantum gravity is not a missing theory,\\
but a structural problem of convergence.
\end{quote}
\medskip

It concerns how different paths of approach
lead to the same boundary.

\subsection{Unified Structure}

These theories are not independent.

\medskip
\begin{quote}
These theories do not contradict one another.\\
They occupy different positions within the same structure.
\end{quote}
\medskip

Quantum field theory approaches through scale.
Relativity approaches through velocity and curvature.

Black holes mark explicit contact.
String theory explores coherence near the limit.

Quantum gravity expresses the difficulty
of understanding their convergence.

\medskip
\begin{quote}
All theories are different responses to the same boundary.
\end{quote}
