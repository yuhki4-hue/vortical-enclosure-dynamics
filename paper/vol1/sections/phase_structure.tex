\section{Phase Structure}
\label{sec:phase}

The dynamics of the causal log network do not produce uniform behavior.
Depending on the magnitude of the effective coupling, the network
operates in qualitatively different regimes.
Physical laws are properties of a specific phase,
not universal principles valid across all parameter regimes.

\subsection{The Control Parameter}

The key parameter is:

\begin{equation}
  \alpha_i = \lambda\,h_i,
  \qquad
  h_i = \sum_j H_{ij},
  \label{eq:alpha}
\end{equation}

where $\lambda$ is the selection sensitivity and $h_i$ is the total
incoming causal history at node $i$.
The value $\alpha_i = 1$ marks the proposed threshold in this model's phase
picture. Self-sustained log density and structure formation above it are
candidate behaviors, not consequences established here: fixed-point existence,
update semantics, and dynamical stability remain open.
The proposed phase structure is shown in Fig.~\ref{fig:phase_structure}.

\subsection{Candidate Phase Interpretation of Effective Laws}

Within the proposed classical-active interpretation, the following structures
are candidate stable features:

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Conservation laws}: $\sigma \approx 0$, so
    $\partial_t\rho + \nabla\cdot\mathbf{J} \approx 0$.
  \item \textbf{Gauge symmetries}: closure configurations stabilize with
    well-defined internal degrees of freedom (Vol.~2).
  \item \textbf{Effective geometry}: under the proposed partial coarse
    correspondence, $d_{ij}$ is represented approximately by a smooth metric
    tensor $g_{\mu\nu}$ (Vol.~3); reconstruction, convergence, and uniqueness
    of this bridge remain open.
\end{itemize}

\begin{keybox}
\textit{Conservation, symmetry, and geometry are modeled as properties of the
classical-active phase rather than as axioms. Their physical phase
identification remains open.}
\end{keybox}

\subsection{Extreme Regimes as Phase Transitions}

The framework proposes model interpretations of extreme regimes:

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Big Bang candidate}: a proposed transition from subcritical to
    active phase as $\alpha$ crosses the critical threshold.
  \item \textbf{Black-hole-interior candidate}: a proposed transition from
    classical-active to non-equilibrium behavior where fixed-point conditions
    break down and $\sigma>0$ is used (Vol.~3).
\end{itemize}
