\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We have presented a structural framework proposing time-like, spatial, and
persistence constructions from a single axiom:
\textit{there is difference}.

The central organizing summary is the compact navigation mnemonic~\cref{eq:chain},
in which the causal log matrix $C_{ij}$, defined by the
self-referential equation~\cref{eq:Cij}, is used to construct or motivate:
a node-local accumulation quantity $\tau$ (\cref{eq:tau}),
network distance $d_{ij}$ (\cref{eq:distance}),
log-flow $J$ (\cref{eq:flow}),
vorticity $\omega$ (\cref{eq:vorticity}),
closure density $L$ (\cref{eq:closure_density}),
and a phenomenological mass-like scaling for candidate persistent structures
(\cref{eq:mass}). Physical identifications remain separate from these formal
constructions. The edges in the mnemonic include definitions, a
weighted-network construction, an effective relation, and an OPEN
discrete-to-continuum bridge; they do not form one uniform physical-generation
rule.

The system exhibits four phases (\cref{tab:phases}).
The observable universe is compared with the classical-active phase, in which
conservation laws and effective geometry are proposed as stable features.
That physical identification and its universality remain open.
