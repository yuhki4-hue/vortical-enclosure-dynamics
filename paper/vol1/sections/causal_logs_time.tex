\section{Causal Logs and Local Time-Like Accumulation}
\label{sec:time}

\subsection{History and Structure}

We distinguish two directed relations between nodes $i$ and $j$:

\begin{itemize}
  \item $H_{ij} \geq 0$: the \textbf{causal history} ---
    total accumulated influence from $i$ to $j$, irreversible and unfiltered.
  \item $C_{ij} \in [0,1)$: the \textbf{causal log} ---
    the selected portion of that history that becomes persistent structure.
    $C_{ij}$ represents the strength of retained influence:
    not what happened, but what continues to matter.
\end{itemize}

\begin{remark}[Source-local terminology]
Throughout Vol.~1, $C_{ij}$ denotes the causal-log matrix defined above.
Some repository navigation records use the same glyph for a closure-degree
or first-formal-carrier variable and describe the causal log separately.
No identity map between those source-specific uses is asserted in this volume.
\end{remark}

The fundamental distinction is:
\begin{equation}
  H_{ij} \neq C_{ij}.
  \label{eq:HneqC}
\end{equation}

The world is not the sum of everything that occurred,
but the structure of what was retained.

\subsection{The Self-Referential Selection Equation}

Selection is not externally imposed. It is generated by the structure itself.
Define the \textbf{log density} at node $i$:

\begin{equation}
  \rho_i = \sum_j C_{ij}.
  \label{eq:rho}
\end{equation}

The \textbf{causal log equation} is:

\begin{keybox}
\begin{equation}
  C_{ij} = 1 - \exp(-\lambda\,\rho_i\,H_{ij}),
  \label{eq:Cij}
\end{equation}
\end{keybox}

\noindent
where $\lambda > 0$ is the selection sensitivity, the sole external parameter.

Substituting \cref{eq:rho} into \cref{eq:Cij} yields a self-referential
fixed-point equation in $\rho_i$:

\[
  \rho_i = \sum_j \left[1 - \exp(-\lambda\,\rho_i\,H_{ij})\right].
\]

Candidate stable fixed points $\rho_i^*$, if separately established, may be
used as model candidates for persistent structure.  A physical identification
requires a separate stability and observation map.

For equation-role purposes, the displayed equations form a constitutive
self-consistency relation and a proposed fixed-point target on node-indexed
quantities. They are not, by themselves, a specified update map. Their display
does not establish existence, uniqueness, convergence of an iteration, or
stability of a solution; those properties require separate conditions.

\begin{remark}
The self-referential structure is not circular.
It admits an iterative interpretation in which existing structure ($\rho_i$)
governs how new history ($H_{ij}$) is selected into new structure ($C_{ij}$).
No particular update schedule or convergence result is specified by that
interpretation.
Structure generates its own selectivity.
\end{remark}

\subsection{Local Time-Like Accumulation}

The node-local quantity $\tau_i$ is not introduced as a background clock. It is
defined relative to the derivative / ordering parameter $t$ as follows:

\begin{remark}[Derivative parameter]
In this Vol.~1 formulation, $t$ denotes the ordering / derivative parameter
used to write the following relation and the dot derivatives below. This
notation does not identify $t$ with $\tau_i$, with physical clock time, or
with the effective external $t$ used in IFGT.
\end{remark}

\begin{keybox}
\begin{equation}
  \frac{d\tau_i}{dt} = \rho_i.
  \label{eq:tau}
\end{equation}
\end{keybox}

This equation is the source-defined node-local time-like accumulation
relation relative to the derivative parameter $t$. Conditional on
$\rho_i\geq0$, it makes $\tau_i$ non-decreasing with respect to that
parameter. The equation alone does not identify $\tau_i$ with a physical
clock reading or establish physical-time emergence.

\noindent
The following mathematical consequences and interpretation limits are kept
separate:

\begin{itemize}
  \item Since $\rho_i \geq 0$ by construction, $\tau_i$ is monotonically
    non-decreasing with respect to $t$.
  \item Where $\rho_i = 0$, the defined quantity $\tau_i$ does not advance
    with respect to $t$.
  \item This monotonicity does not by itself establish state
    non-reconstructibility, thermodynamic irreversibility, or a physical arrow
    of time.
\end{itemize}

\begin{keybox}
\textit{$\tau_i$ is a node-local accumulation / ordering quantity.
Its interpretation as physical time remains proposed rather than established.}
\end{keybox}

\subsection{The Log-Generation Rate}

Taking the time derivative of $\rho_i$ using \cref{eq:Cij} yields:

\begin{equation}
  \dot{\rho}_i = \frac{\lambda\,\rho_i\,B_i}{1 - \lambda\,A_i},
  \label{eq:rhodot}
\end{equation}

where $A_i = \sum_j e^{-\lambda\rho_i H_{ij}} H_{ij}$
and $B_i = \sum_j e^{-\lambda\rho_i H_{ij}} \dot{H}_{ij}$.
The numerator $\lambda\rho_i B_i$ measures the rate of new history injection;
the denominator $1 - \lambda A_i$ captures saturation by existing structure.
This microscopic expression is proposed as an antecedent of the Vol.~1
continuum log-generation rate $\sigma$ appearing in \cref{sec:flow}.
No microscopic-to-continuum projection or identity equation is fixed here.
