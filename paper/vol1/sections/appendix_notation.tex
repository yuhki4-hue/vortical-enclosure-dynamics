\section{Notation and Symbol Reference}
\label{app:notation}

The following table summarizes the principal symbols used in this volume.

\begin{table}[h]
\centering
\begin{tabular}{lll}
\toprule
Symbol & Definition & Introduced \\
\midrule
$H_{ij}$ & Causal history ($i \to j$ influence strength) & \cref{sec:time} \\
$C_{ij}$ & Causal log (selected structure) & \cref{eq:Cij} \\
$\rho_i$ & Log density $= \sum_j C_{ij}$ & \cref{eq:rho} \\
$\lambda$ & Selection sensitivity (sole external parameter) & \cref{eq:Cij} \\
$\tau_i$ & Observational time ($d\tau_i/dt = \rho_i$) & \cref{eq:tau} \\
$t$ & Ordering parameter (not observational time) & \cref{sec:time} \\
$S_{ij}$ & Symmetric component of $C_{ij}$ (spatial) & \cref{eq:decomp} \\
$A_{ij}$ & Antisymmetric component of $C_{ij}$ (causal) & \cref{eq:decomp} \\
$\tilde{S}_{ij}$ & Maximum-path propagation strength & \cref{sec:space} \\
$d_{ij}$ & Spatial distance $= -\log\tilde{S}_{ij}$ & \cref{eq:distance} \\
$g_{\mu\nu}$ & Effective metric tensor (emergent) & \cref{eq:metric} \\
$J_{ij}$ & Log-flow $= C_{ij} - C_{ji}$ & \cref{eq:flow} \\
$\sigma$ & Log-generation rate & \cref{eq:continuity} \\
$\omega$ & Vorticity $= \nabla \times \mathbf{J}$ & \cref{eq:vorticity} \\
$L$ & Closure density $\sim |\omega|$ & \cref{eq:closure_density} \\
$L_{\max}$ & Complete-closure limit & \cref{eq:prohibition} \\
$B(L)$ & Effective non-closure barrier & \cref{eq:barrier} \\
$\kappa_B$ & Effective barrier strength & \cref{eq:barrier} \\
$E(A)$ & Closure energy of configuration $A$ & \cref{eq:closure_energy} \\
$\Delta_k$ & Closure distance $= L_{\max} - L_k$ & \cref{eq:delta} \\
$m_k$ & Effective mass scaling $\propto \kappa_B / \Delta_k^2$ & \cref{eq:mass} \\
$\alpha_i$ & Control parameter $= \lambda h_i$ & \cref{eq:alpha} \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\caption{Symbol reference for Vol.~1.}
\label{tab:notation}
\end{table}
