\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

This volume has shown that gravitational dynamics can be interpreted as
arising from closure geometry.
Beginning from the modified distance definition
$w_{ab} = -\log S_{ab} + \epsilon L_{ab}$,
we have identified:

\begin{itemize}
  \item modified distance as a candidate gravity-like description
        (Section~\ref{sec:gravity_distance});
  \item the closure tensor $L_{\mu\nu}$ as a proposed contribution to metric distortion
        (Section~\ref{sec:closure_density});
  \item the Einstein equation as an intended weak-field comparison target
        (Section~\ref{sec:einstein});
  \item a Schwarzschild-type exterior as a structural comparison target
        (Section~\ref{sec:schwarzschild});
  \item the black hole interior as a non-equilibrium closure phase with $\sigma > 0$
        (Section~\ref{sec:black_hole});
  \item cosmic expansion as the global $\sigma > 0$ regime,
        structurally analogous to the Friedmann equation
        (Section~\ref{sec:cosmology}).
\end{itemize}

The following display is the program's compact navigation mnemonic:
\begin{equation}
  \text{Difference}
  \;\to\; C_{ij}
  \;\to\; J
  \;\to\; \omega
  \;\to\; L
  \;\to\; g_{\mu\nu}
  \;\to\; \text{Einstein equation}
  \;\to\; \text{Universe}.
\end{equation}

Its arrows have distinct roles: $C_{ij}\to J_{ij}$ is a discrete definition;
$J_{ij}\to\mathbf J(x)$ and the closure-carrier transitions are OPEN bridges;
$\mathbf J\to\omega$ is a definition; $\omega\to L$ is an effective relation;
the metric endpoint is partial and bridge-dependent; and the Einstein and
Universe endpoints are structural comparisons or proposed physical
interpretations.  The mnemonic is therefore not a uniform derivation or
generation mechanism.

Taken together with Vol.~1 and Vol.~2, this gives a connected program of formal
constructions and proposed interpretations for time, space, matter, and
gravity beginning from the axiom \emph{there is difference}.  It is not an
established physical derivation of those targets.
It should not be read as final closure of the framework.
Vol.~4 further clarifies why complete closure appears as a descriptive limit
rather than as an attainable state.

The interpretation is structural and qualitative in nature,
and does not yet constitute a predictive theory.
Quantitative correspondence with observation remains a goal for future work.
\begin{equation}
  \boxed{\text{The world is not closed. It exists by never fully closing.}}
\end{equation}
