Vortical Enclosure Dynamics (VED) proposes formal constructions and physical
interpretations beginning from a single axiom:
\emph{there is difference}.
Vol.~1 introduced the causal log $C_{ij}$ and time-like and spatial candidate
constructions.  Vol.~2 proposed structural correspondences between closure
geometry and Standard-Model organization.

This volume addresses gravity.
The central claim is that gravity is not a new force,
but a modification of the definition of distance.
Incorporating closure density $L_{ab}$ into the path cost supplies a formal
program relating a metric fixed-point target, weak-field comparisons,
non-equilibrium closure phases, and a global $\sigma>0$ cosmological regime.
The physical identifications and exact recoveries remain proposed rather than
established.

No additional structures are introduced beyond the closure framework.
The present theory does not yet possess quantitative predictive power;
structural organization is the primary goal.
