\section{Intelligence as a Second Attractor}

This section presents a forward conceptual proposal in which intelligence is treated not as a direct extension of life, but as a second attractor supported by the proposed life-attractor description. This separates the candidate conditions for life from additional conditions considered for intelligence.

\subsection{Position of Intelligence}

Intelligence is often treated as an advanced function of living systems. Within the terminology proposed here, life is described through an attractor region, while many living systems persist without developing intelligence. Intelligence is therefore not modeled merely as a strengthening of that region.

\subsection{Second Attractor}

Intelligence appears when information is not only flowing, but also retained, referenced, and reused. Past states influence present states, and present states modify future trajectories. This creates recurrent loops of information flow.

Such loops are proposed to require conditions that differ from those considered for life alone. Intelligence is therefore interpreted as a candidate second attractor supported by, but not identical to, the life-attractor description.

\subsection{Structural Independence}

Formally, one may write
\begin{equation}
  \Omega_{\mathrm{intel}} \subset \Omega_{\mathrm{life}},
\end{equation}
but the internal structure of \(\Omega_{\mathrm{intel}}\) is proposed to depend on additional constraints. Candidate conditions include persistent reference to state history, temporal accumulation of information, and sharing of information across multiple components. Their sufficiency and their biological or cognitive translation are not established in the present Bio model.

\subsection{Probability Structure}

Following Section 8, the emergence of intelligence is treated as the satisfaction of conditions distinct from the emergence of life. Its probability therefore has a conditional structure. A schematic factorization is
\begin{equation}
  P_{\mathrm{intel}} \approx P_{\mathrm{life}}\,P_{\mathrm{second}\mid\mathrm{life}},
\end{equation}
where \(P_{\mathrm{second}\mid\mathrm{life}}\) denotes the probability of reaching the second attractor given the life attractor. This expression is not a numerical estimate. It records the two-stage structure of the problem.

\subsection{Non-Teleological Interpretation}

Treating intelligence as a proposed second attractor does not give it a privileged status. An attractor is not a goal. Here it names a candidate structure that would persist when its conditions are satisfied. Intelligence is therefore neither inevitable nor a final form in this conceptual account.

\subsection{Relation to Internal Time}

In the notation of Section 7, life is a structure in which \(\tau\) continues to progress. Intelligence is characterized by the reference and reuse of the history of \(\tau\). In this sense, intelligence is not merely flow. It is recursive information flow.

\subsection{Summary}

Intelligence is not treated as a simple extension of life. It is proposed as a recursive information-flow structure supported by the life-attractor description. This forward interpretation motivates additional conditions; it does not establish a stable second-attractor implementation or a cognitive translation.

\subsection{Connection to Further Work}

The next question is how this second attractor is implemented. Its realization may involve memory, temporal integration, language, and inter-individual sharing. These issues are beyond the minimal biological framework developed here.
