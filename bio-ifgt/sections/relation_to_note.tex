\section{Relation to Note}

This section clarifies the proposed relation between this paper and the corresponding note-style exposition. The aim is not to summarize the theory, but to compare analogous roles in different descriptive layers.

\subsection{Difference in Descriptive Layer}

This paper is written as an L3 formal layer. The note essay is written as an L1 intuitive layer. They are proposed to concern a related target, but their purposes differ. The paper fixes definitions, equations, and relations. The note essay supports intuitive understanding through accessible language and metaphor. The exact L1 source/version locator is not fixed here.

The relevant relation is therefore a proposed structural correspondence, not identity of objects, dynamics, or wording and not an exact translation.

\subsection{Correspondences}

The concepts used in this paper correspond to intuitive expressions in the note essay:
\begin{align}
  \I &\leftrightarrow \text{local concentration, bias, or structured region},\\
  \J &\leftrightarrow \text{flow, repetition, or ease of passage},\\
  \PhiI &\leftrightarrow \text{return tendency or stable place},\\
  \text{attractor} &\leftrightarrow \text{returning to the same region or preserving form}.
\end{align}
These are not direct translations. They are candidate analogies between differently typed descriptions; no exact mapping or empirical identity of phenomena is established.

\subsection{Development and Form}

The treatment of development and morphogenesis in Section 6 corresponds to the note-level idea that repeated changes form flows and that flows stabilize into form. Both descriptions avoid taking design or purpose as primitives.

\subsection{Definition of Life}

Section 7 proposes a life-attractor terminology based on sustained motion in a bounded region. In the note essay, this appears as the idea that life is not an object but a state. The attractor reading is conditional; it does not supply a stability result or an operational biological life criterion.

\subsection{Probability Revision}

The probability revision in Section 8 corresponds to the note-level statement that life is not best understood as a miracle-like exact hit. The shared structure is the shift from a point target to a region, and from exact matching to arrival and persistence.

\subsection{Connection to Intelligence}

The proposed treatment of intelligence as a second attractor in Section 9 corresponds to a note-level description of a flow that appears to think. Neither source supplies a stable second-attractor implementation; the conceptual distinction between life and intelligence is retained.

\subsection{Relation to Appendices}

Intermediate appendices can bridge the note essay and this paper. They may introduce state, flow, and stability with minimal mathematics. Future versions can be updated to reflect the structure fixed here.

\subsection{Summary}

This paper and the note essay use different descriptive forms. The paper fixes the Bio-IFGT formal structure, while the note essay supplies an intuitive presentation. Their proposed correspondence can improve coherence across layers, but it is not an identity or a typed translation and remains dependent on fixing the exact L1 source/version.
