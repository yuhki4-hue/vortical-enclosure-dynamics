# Three Proposed Claims Worth Examining

This note preserves the stronger summary claims that previously appeared in the top-level README.

## 1. Conservation may be phase-relative in the VED model.

The base equation is $\partial_t\rho + \nabla\cdot J = \sigma$ with $\sigma > 0$.  
Conservation ($\sigma = 0$) is proposed as an effective regime of the classical
fixed-point phase.  Within the source-local VED continuum description,
$\sigma>0$ denotes a positive log-source regime and is associated with
accumulation / temporal ordering.  It is not an established necessary-and-
sufficient criterion for physical-time generation.

## 2. Newton's constant $G$ is treated as an effective target.

$$G \sim \frac{\eta_\phi , \epsilon}{4\pi , \kappa_{B,\mathrm{eff}}^2} , F(\lambda, \beta, \ldots)$$

where $\kappa_{B,\mathrm{eff}}$ is an effective non-closure scale and $\epsilon$ is the closure-to-distance coupling.
Universality of $G$ across particle species is a proposed coarse-graining
target, not an established dynamic-convergence result.

## 3. Black-hole interiors receive a proposed phase interpretation.

The interior is a phase, not a geometric extension. As $r \to 0$:

$$B(L) = -\kappa_{B,\mathrm{eff}}\log\!\left(1-\frac{L}{L_{\max}}\right) \to \infty$$

Within the effective barrier model, complete closure is represented as an
inaccessible limit.  Identifying the center with that phase boundary remains a
candidate physical interpretation.
