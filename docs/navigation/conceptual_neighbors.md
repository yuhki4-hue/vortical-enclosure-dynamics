# Conceptual Neighbors

This document helps route common interpretations of VED into nearby conceptual
traditions without reducing VED to any one of them. It is a navigation aid, not
a new theory layer.

## Why This Document Exists

VED shares vocabulary and concerns with several existing traditions: complex
systems, dissipative structures, autopoiesis, enactivism, FEP, Predictive
Processing, IIT, and Global Workspace-type theories.

Many readers first encounter VED through complex systems, autopoiesis,
dissipative structures, or enactivism. These are often the correct first
neighbors, but they are not the intended final category.

These overlaps are legitimate. The routing problem is reduction. VED attempts
to extend a common generative vocabulary across multiple descriptive layers:
physical, biological, cognitive, consciousness, intelligence, and social
stopping-operation layers. It should therefore not be collapsed into a single
neighboring category.

For the core ordering of VED terms, see [Generative Sequence](generative_sequence.md).
For the strongest complex-systems / dissipative-structure reduction trap, see
[Not Just Complex Systems](not_just_complex_systems.md).

## Complex Systems

### Why Readers Connect Them

Readers may connect VED to complex systems because VED discusses emergence,
nonlinear organization, feedback, attractor-like behavior, and multi-layer
structure.

### Points of Overlap

- emergent structure,
- feedback and coupling,
- multi-scale organization,
- attractor-like stabilization,
- non-equilibrium maintenance.

Relevant links:

- [Not Just Complex Systems](not_just_complex_systems.md)
- [Bio-IFGT life attractor](../../bio-ifgt/sections/life_attractor.tex)
- [Concept Map](concept_map.md)
- [Reading Paths](reading_paths.md)

### Key Differences

Complex systems theory is a broad field for studying interacting systems and
emergent behavior. VED uses a more specific generative sequence: difference,
gradient, flow, vortex, enclosure, and non-closure.

VED can be read as neighboring complex systems theory, but it should not be
reduced to "complex systems" as a category label.

### Interpretation Boundaries

Do not read this relation as claiming that:

- VED is only complex systems theory,
- all VED terms already have standard complex-systems equivalents,
- VED's physical, IFGT, consciousness, and intelligence layers are exhausted by
  complexity vocabulary.

## Dissipative Structures

### Why Readers Connect Them

Readers may connect VED to dissipative structures because VED discusses
non-equilibrium persistence, dissipation, flow, and maintained structure.

### Points of Overlap

- non-equilibrium structure,
- persistence through flow,
- dissipation as structurally relevant,
- stabilization without static equilibrium.

Relevant links:

- [Not Just Complex Systems](not_just_complex_systems.md)
- [Bio-IFGT life attractor](../../bio-ifgt/sections/life_attractor.tex)
- [Vol.4 dynamic barrier](../../paper/vol4/sections/dynamic_barrier.tex)
- [Simulation Reading Guide](simulation_reading_guide.md)

### Key Differences

Dissipative-structure readings are closest where VED discusses biological
maintenance and dynamic barriers. But VED is not limited to dissipative
structure theory. It also treats causal logs, non-closure, projection,
consciousness as log referencing, and intelligence as externalized stopping.

### Interpretation Boundaries

Do not read this relation as claiming that:

- VED is only a theory of dissipative structures,
- the Vol.4 simulations are biological dissipative-structure simulations,
- dissipation alone explains enclosure, consciousness, or intelligence in VED.

## Autopoiesis

### Why Readers Connect Them

Readers may connect VED to autopoiesis because Bio-IFGT treats life as a
sustained quasi-closure regime and because VED often describes maintenance,
boundary formation, and self-sustaining organization.

### Points of Overlap

- life as ongoing maintenance,
- boundary and organization,
- organism-environment interaction,
- self-sustaining structure rather than completed object.

Relevant links:

- [Not Just Complex Systems](not_just_complex_systems.md)
- [Bio-IFGT life attractor](../../bio-ifgt/sections/life_attractor.tex)
- [Bio-IFGT formal mapping](../../bio-ifgt/sections/formal_mapping.tex)
- [Enactivism Comparison](enactivism_comparison.md)

### Key Differences

Autopoiesis begins from living systems and their self-producing organization.
VED begins from differential generative structure and then reads biological
quasi-closure as one layer within a broader sequence.

In short:

```text
Autopoiesis: life -> self-maintaining organization
VED: difference -> enclosure/non-closure -> life as one quasi-closure layer
```

### Interpretation Boundaries

Do not read this relation as claiming that:

- VED is an autopoiesis theory,
- autopoiesis is reducible to VED,
- all VED layers are biological self-maintenance,
- self-maintenance alone explains the physical and social layers.

## Enactivism

Enactivism is a close biological-cognitive neighbor. VED overlaps with
enactivism around organism-environment coupling, anti-static representation
stance, process, and maintenance. The difference is that enactivism usually
begins from embodied cognition, while VED begins from differential generative
structure.

See: [Enactivism Comparison](enactivism_comparison.md).

## Free Energy Principle

FEP is a close neighbor for questions of stability, maintenance, constraint,
and minimization. VED can be read alongside FEP where biological and cognitive
systems maintain viable regimes, but VED does not define itself through
free-energy minimization.

See: [FEP Comparison](fep_comparison.md).

## Predictive Processing

Predictive Processing is a close neighbor for prediction, updating, error
signals, and fast-layer dynamics. VED / IFGT uses these themes in the
consciousness paper, but the focus is temporal non-closure and log referencing
rather than prediction alone.

See: [Predictive Processing Comparison](predictive_processing_comparison.md).

## Consciousness Theories

IIT and Global Workspace-type theories are neighboring consciousness
frameworks. VED consciousness is not primarily defined by integration quantity,
global broadcasting, self-representation, or recursive self-modeling. The
navigation emphasis is temporal non-closure, cross-scale log referencing,
speed-scale orchestration, and, in the broader intelligence-layer context,
externalized stopping structures.
