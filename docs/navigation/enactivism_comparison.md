# Enactivism Comparison

This page is a navigation aid for readers who approach VED from enactivism,
embodied cognition, ecological psychology, autopoiesis, or organism-environment
coupling. It does not claim that VED is an enactivist theory, nor that
enactivism is reducible to VED.

## Why This Comparison Matters

Enactivism-oriented readers are likely to notice themes that also appear in the
VED / IFGT tree:

- cognition is not cleanly separable from environment,
- static representation is not sufficient as a complete account,
- process and coupling matter more than isolated objects,
- living systems must be read through ongoing maintenance rather than completed
  form.

These themes are closest to the repository areas where IFGT, Bio-IFGT,
consciousness, and intelligence discuss quasi-closure, environment response,
log referencing, and externalized stopping.

## Agreement

VED and enactivism are close in several broad orientations.

| Shared Orientation | VED / IFGT Reading |
|---|---|
| Process over static object | Structure is read as dynamically generated and maintained. |
| Organism-environment inseparability | Biological and cognitive quasi-closures form through interaction with their environment. |
| Anti-static representation stance | VED emphasizes trace, flow, constraint, log referencing, and stopping operations rather than fixed internal representations alone. |
| Maintenance over completed form | Life and consciousness are treated as sustained regimes, not completed objects. |

## Difference

The starting point is different.

| Enactivism | VED |
|---|---|
| Begins from embodied cognition, life, organism, and environment. | Begins from difference, gradient, flow, vortex, enclosure, and non-closure. |
| Primarily addresses biological and cognitive sense-making. | Uses one differential-development vocabulary across physical, biological, cognitive, and social layers. |
| Often treats organism-environment coupling as the central domain. | Treats organism-environment coupling as one biological-cognitive realization of broader enclosure/non-closure dynamics. |

In short:

```text
Enactivism: life -> cognition
VED: difference -> life -> cognition
```

This contrast is only a navigation guide. It does not settle detailed
compatibility with any specific enactivist author or school.

## Possible Relation

Within this repository, enactivism can be read as a biological-cognitive
neighbor of VED rather than as a rival theory. A cautious formulation is:

> Enactivism may be interpreted as a biological-cognitive realization of broader
> enclosure/non-closure dynamics, especially where organism-environment coupling
> supplies the conditions for quasi-closure, response, and stopping.

This is a positioning statement for navigation. It is not yet a full comparative
paper.

## Interpretation Boundaries

Do not read this comparison as claiming that:

- VED proves enactivism,
- enactivism is reducible to VED,
- all enactivist concepts already have formal VED equivalents,
- organism-environment coupling exhausts VED,
- VED has already written a complete account of autopoiesis or enaction.

Current status: explicit bridge written at the navigation level; full theory
comparison remains future work.
