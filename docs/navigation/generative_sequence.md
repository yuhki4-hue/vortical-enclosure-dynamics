# Generative Sequence — Role-Separated Navigation

This document is the canonical human-readable navigation record for VED's
staged formal architecture. It preserves the project's question of generation
without assigning one meaning to every arrow in a compact sequence.

Machine-readable calibration remains in the [AI Spine](../ai_spine/README.md).
Its generated ranking views are synchronized with this role-separated account
as navigation metadata; they do not override the relation statuses below.

## Reading rule

VED begins from the working statement:

> There is difference.

The relations used after that statement do not all have the same logical
status:

$$
\text{navigation order}
\neq
\text{proof order}
\neq
\text{formal dependency}
\neq
\text{physical generation}.
$$

An arrow in a compact summary is therefore a reading aid only. Its local role
must be read from the table below. Domain labels are documentary labels, not
new formal objects or ontological commitments.

## Source-identity scope

In some navigation and overview records, $C_{ij}$ names a closure-degree /
rank-1 formal carrier. Vol.1 uses the same glyph for the causal log selected
from $H_{ij}$. No identity map between those source-specific objects is fixed
here. The scalar $L$, edge quantity $L_{ab}$, and tensorial $L_{\mu\nu}$ are
also separate carrier-specific objects unless a bridge is supplied.

## Layered navigation

The table should be read in four layers rather than as one seamless derivation.

| Layer | Included relations | Allowed inference | Forbidden inference |
|---|---|---|---|
| A — formal / model-local constructions | N01–N02, N04–N08, N10, N12, and formal parts of N15–N17 | the stated quantity or target is organized, defined, or constructed in its declared domain | the construction is automatically a physical mechanism or emergence result |
| B — representation changes / bridges | N09, N13, N14, N18–N22 | a correspondence or coarse endpoint is proposed and can be audited | the missing map, limit, tensorization, identity, or equivalence has been supplied |
| C — effective / phenomenological relations | N03, N11, and effective parts of N15–N17 | the relation can organize a scoped model or comparison | universality, fundamental necessity, or exact physical recovery |
| D — physical interpretations | time, enclosure, particle, space, gravity / spacetime, cross-theory unity | these remain proposed interpretations or open research targets | that the preceding formal sequence proves physical emergence |

## How to read compact chains elsewhere

Historical and compact summaries may display sequences such as
$C\to J\to\omega\to L\to g$. They are navigation mnemonics, not a typed
calculus and not a claim that each edge is a causal generation mechanism.
Read each edge through the canonical table:

- causal-log $C_{ij}$ to $J_{ij}$: discrete definition;
- $J_{ij}$ to $\mathbf J(x)$: informal / OPEN bridge;
- $\mathbf J(x)$ to $\omega$: continuum definition;
- $\omega$ to scalar $L$: effective proportional relation;
- scalar or edge closure quantities to $L_{\mu\nu}$: OPEN carrier bridge;
- network or closure quantities to $g_{\mu\nu}$: partial / OPEN bridge and
  proposed physical interpretation.
- $\rho_i$ to $\rho(x,t)$ and $\dot\rho_i$ to $\sigma$: informal / proposed
  continuum correspondences whose projections remain OPEN;
- source-local VED quantities to IFGT fields: proposed correspondences, with
  the matrix-to-field, temporal, and source-term maps OPEN.

Likewise, $\rho_i\to\tau_i$ is an accumulation definition, whereas physical
time is a proposed interpretation; $S_{ij}\to d_{ij}$ is a weighted-network
construction, whereas physical space remains open.

## Generation vocabulary

The word “generation” is retained as the project's organizing question, but
it is scoped locally:

- formal generation refers only to a stated construction or update;
- a candidate generative mechanism is not an established mechanism;
- physical emergence remains proposed or OPEN unless its evidence burden is
  separately met;
- navigation order records how to read the project, not ontological priority.

## Quick self-check

- Does one arrow have one universal meaning? **No; inspect its local role.**
- Does a formal definition prove physical emergence? **No.**
- Does a coarse endpoint close a bridge? **No; N09, N13, N14, and N18–N22
  remain OPEN at the relevant transitions.**
- Is navigation $C_{ij}$ identical to Vol.1 causal-log $C_{ij}$? **Not without
  an explicit map.**
- Is “generative sequence” a proof of a distinct generative equation? **No;
  that equation class remains NOT ESTABLISHED.**
