# FEP Comparison

This page is a navigation aid for readers who approach VED from the Free Energy
Principle (FEP). It does not claim that VED is an FEP theory, nor that FEP is
reducible to VED.

## Why This Comparison Matters

FEP-oriented readers are likely to notice themes that also appear in the VED /
IFGT tree:

- stability is not a static condition but a maintained regime,
- biological and cognitive systems must resist uncontrolled dissipation,
- constraints shape the range of viable trajectories,
- prediction, updating, and sedimentation can affect future dynamics.

These themes are closest to the repository areas where Bio-IFGT discusses life
as sustained non-equilibrium structure, and where the consciousness paper
compares its account with Predictive Processing and Free Energy approaches.

## Agreement

VED / IFGT and FEP can be read as sharing several broad concerns.

| Shared Orientation | VED / IFGT Reading |
|---|---|
| Stability requires maintenance | Stable structure is dynamically sustained rather than simply given. |
| Constraint matters | Constraint fields and potentials shape possible flow and persistence. |
| Dissipation matters | Uncontrolled dissipation prevents stable quasi-closure or log referencing. |
| Prediction is not isolated | Prediction interacts with slower sedimented structure and future behavior. |

## Difference

The explanatory center is different.

| FEP | VED / IFGT |
|---|---|
| Often begins from free-energy minimization, inference, and self-maintaining systems. | Begins from difference, gradient, flow, enclosure, and non-closure. |
| Stability is frequently read through minimization of free energy or prediction error. | Stability is read through quasi-closure, constraint, and non-closed enclosure dynamics. |
| The core vocabulary is inference, generative model, Markov blanket, and free energy. | The core vocabulary is difference, flow, vortex, enclosure, non-closure, constraint, and projection. |

The consciousness paper explicitly states that its focus is not the minimization
of free energy itself, but how prediction sediments into a log-referenceable
structure.

## Possible Relation

Within this repository, FEP may be interpreted as one biological-cognitive
neighbor of the VED / IFGT framework. A cautious formulation is:

> FEP can be read as a theory of stability and maintenance within biological and
> cognitive systems, while VED / IFGT re-situates such maintenance within a
> broader differential-development vocabulary of enclosure, non-closure, and
> constraint.

This does not imply identity between the frameworks.

## Interpretation Boundaries

Do not read this comparison as claiming that:

- VED proves FEP,
- FEP is reducible to VED,
- VED replaces FEP,
- free-energy minimization is the sole basis of VED,
- the VED equations are FEP equations under another name,
- the repository already contains a complete formal translation between FEP and
  VED.
