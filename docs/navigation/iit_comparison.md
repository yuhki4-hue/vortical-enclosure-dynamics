# IIT Comparison

This page is a navigation aid for readers who approach VED from Integrated
Information Theory (IIT). It does not claim that VED is an IIT theory, nor that
IIT is reducible to VED.

## Why This Comparison Matters

IIT-oriented readers are likely to notice themes that also appear in the
consciousness paper:

- consciousness is not treated as simple local processing,
- multiple elements form a relational structure,
- integration and coupling matter,
- too little or too much structural coupling can change the regime.

The main repository basis for this comparison is the consciousness paper's
explicit discussion of IIT-type models.

## Agreement

VED / IFGT and IIT can be read as sharing several broad concerns.

| Shared Orientation | VED / IFGT Reading |
|---|---|
| Isolated parts are insufficient | Consciousness is treated as a relation among multiple layers and variables. |
| Structural relation matters | Coupling, viscosity, delay, and meaning gradients jointly shape the regime. |
| Integration-like structure matters | Too little coupling prevents stable log referencing. |
| Pure locality is insufficient | Consciousness is not localized to a single site or quantity. |

## Difference

The explanatory center is different.

| IIT | VED / IFGT |
|---|---|
| Often centers integrated information or structural indivisibility. | Centers temporal non-closure and the opening of log referencing. |
| Integration quantity can become the main explanatory index. | No single quantity is treated as consciousness itself. |
| Strong integration can be read positively. | Excessive integration can become fixation or loss of plasticity. |

The consciousness paper explicitly states that consciousness is not maximum
integration but a non-closure stability window.

## Possible Relation

Within this repository, IIT may be interpreted as a neighboring theory that
emphasizes one important dimension of structural relation. A cautious
formulation is:

> IIT can be read as highlighting the importance of integrated relational
> structure, while VED / IFGT re-situates integration within a finite
> non-closure window where log referencing can open without collapsing into
> fixation or dissipation.

This possible relation does not imply identity between the frameworks.

## Interpretation Boundaries

Do not read this comparison as claiming that:

- VED proves IIT,
- IIT is reducible to VED,
- VED replaces IIT,
- integrated information is identical to IFGT information,
- consciousness is defined by a single integration quantity in VED,
- the repository already contains a complete formal translation between IIT and
  VED.

Current status: explicit bridge written at the navigation level; full formal
comparison remains future work.
