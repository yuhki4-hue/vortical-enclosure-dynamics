# Open Problems

This document collects the principal unresolved problems for the current public baseline of VED. The purpose is not to gesture vaguely toward future work, but to define a concrete research agenda that would move the framework from structural coherence toward quantitative responsibility.

## Vol.1 Foundations

### Formal definition of causal log structure

The notion that time is the accumulation of causal logs and space is the structure of causal logs requires a precise mathematical definition of log composition, ordering, coarse-graining, and equivalence.

### Microscopic definition of `C_ij`

The basic relational object `C_ij` must be sharpened. It is still necessary to specify what kind of object it is, what admissibility constraints it obeys, and how its local update rules generate effective currents.

### Emergence of metric-like structure

The step from accumulated closure history to an operational distance definition remains conceptually motivated but not formally closed. The exact conditions under which `g ~ d(L)` becomes a stable geometry must be derived.

### Quantization or emergence of quantum behavior

A major unresolved question is whether quantum mechanics enters as a separate postulate, an effective statistical regime, or an intrinsic feature of closure dynamics. This is central rather than optional.

## Vol.2 Standard Model Correspondence

### Closure classification with particle-level precision

The current correspondence between closure attractors and particle families must be made exact enough to distinguish not only broad classes but specific states, charges, and generation structure.

### Neutrino mass

The framework needs a clear account of how neutrino mass arises, whether by closure imperfection, inter-closure coupling, or another mechanism internal to the theory.

### CP violation

CP violation remains an important test case. A serious theory-level account must explain whether CP asymmetry is fundamental, emergent, or tied to specific closure transitions.

### Quantization of gauge-like behavior

If gauge structure is to emerge from closure geometry, then the relevant quantization conditions and effective field behavior have to be derived rather than inferred by analogy.

## Vol.3 Gravity and Cosmology

### Exact Schwarzschild solution

One major benchmark is whether an exact Schwarzschild solution, or a mathematically equivalent external solution, can be recovered from the VED distance-distortion picture.

### Deriving Newton's constant from microscopic `C_ij` structure

The theory must show how an effective gravitational coupling emerges from microscopic relational structure. Deriving Newton's constant from the underlying causal network is a central target.

### Black hole phase boundaries

If black holes are non-equilibrium closure phases, the phase transition criteria, stability boundaries, and horizon-like behavior must be specified quantitatively.

### Deriving sigma(rho) parameters `xi`, `rho_max`, and `gamma`

The cosmological sector needs a derivation of the effective sigma-density relation and its characteristic parameters, rather than a merely suggestive phenomenological fit.

## Cross-Cutting Problems

### At least one quantitative observable prediction

The framework must produce at least one explicit, testable, quantitative observable that differs in principle from existing theories or constrains its own parameter space.

### Scale bridging

VED must demonstrate a controlled bridge from microscopic closure rules to mesoscopic effective dynamics and then to macroscopic geometry or cosmology.

### Mathematical language choice

It remains open which formal language best carries the framework: discrete network dynamics, topological flow systems, geometric measure theory, categorical structure, or some hybrid.

### Recovering successful legacy limits

Any viable development of the theory must recover the domains in which standard quantum theory, general relativity, and effective field descriptions already work well.

### Criteria for falsification

A serious public theory needs stated failure modes. It should be possible to say what empirical or formal results would count against the framework.

## Immediate Research Priorities

The most urgent near-term tasks are:

1. formalize the microscopic meaning of `C_ij`
2. derive the transition from closure log `L` to geometry `g`
3. identify a candidate route to quantum emergence
4. extract a quantitative gravity benchmark
5. produce at least one observable prediction

Until these are addressed, VED should be read as a coherent structural research program rather than a finished physical theory.
